The fine print

Scope and booking conditions

Prices assume an empty property in ordinary move-out condition.

Floor area, room sizes, window count and access, Wall washing extent and balcony/patio size to be confirmed before final quote.

Extra bathrooms, oversized properties, difficult/high access, heavy contamination and specialist restoration are assessed and seperately quoted before booking.


Standard bathroom cleaning is included in both packages, Specialist Bathroom Detail and Steam Sanitation remain optional upgrades.


Gardening, rubbish removal, driveway pressure washing, furiture/upholstery cleaning, specialist bathroom restoration and glass treatments are outside these packages unless expressly quoted.
